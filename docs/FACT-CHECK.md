## Open conflicts — do not publish either way until resolved

1. **Rosa's current owner.** Sources variously say bred/owned by Alan
   Woodbury, campaigned by Lisa Lockhart, and "under new ownership" for 2026.
   The site currently makes no present-tense ownership claim. Keep it that way
   until you confirm.
2. **Does Mill Iron own Solo Select?** One snippet read "the Bielenys, who own
   Solo Select Horses." Mill Iron is in Czar, Alberta; Solo Select is in
   Gainesville, Texas. This is probably a search-engine conflation. Confirm the
   actual relationship — it changes how the two brands should be presented.
